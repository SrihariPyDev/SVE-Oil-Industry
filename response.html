Who We Are

Trusted Lubricant
Partner Since 2007

Sri Venkateswara Enterprises was founded in 2007 with a clear purpose: to be a dependable, customer-focused distributor of industrial oils and lubricants serving the manufacturing and industrial sectors of Tamil Nadu.

Over the years, our team — comprising experienced professionals with approximately 25 years of collective industry expertise — has built relationships rooted in trust, technical understanding, and consistent product supply. We understand that the right lubricant, delivered on time, is critical to keeping industrial operations running without interruption.

Our portfolio spans a comprehensive range of industrial lubricants from hydraulic oils and gear oils to specialty greases and transformer oils. We partner with leading brands including APAR, SERVO, Bharat Petroleum, Klüber Lubrication, Castrol, Mobil, and Mosil to bring you quality products suited to a wide range of industrial applications.

At Sri Venkateswara Enterprises, we go beyond simple supply. Our experienced team is available around the clock to provide guidance on product selection, application-specific recommendations, and responsive customer support.
